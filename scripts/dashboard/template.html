Trading Bot Dashboard

How the bot's own paper trades are doing: account value, open positions, closed trades and the decisions behind them. Data comes from the Alpaca paper account and the bot's records on your Mac.

Account value

Daily closing value of the paper account.

Road to live trading

The bot's own rule: at least 100 closed trades, a win rate whose 95% lower bound is 62% or more, and a positive average result.

Drop from the peak

How far the account is below its best value. The bot pauses new trades at 10%.

Open positions

Stocks the bot holds right now, with the broker stop-loss that protects each one.

Closed trades

Every finished trade. R is the result measured in units of the risk taken: +1R means it made what it risked, −1R means the stop was hit.

Results by stock

Closed trades grouped by stock.

Trade results in R

How many trades ended at each result.

What the bot considered

Every setup the bot looked at, whether it traded, and why not. "If taken" is the simulated result the bot records for setups it skipped, so you can see what the filters cost or saved.

Broker orders

Orders on the Alpaca paper account, newest first.
